You don't need to read this page to work with Jaxtech — most people never do. But if you want to know what you're actually buying, and why it behaves differently to anything off a shelf, here it is without the marketing.

The controller

Every Jaxtech installation is built around a dedicated piece of hardware that lives on your property — not an app talking to someone else's data centre. It's the difference between a home that depends on the internet and one that merely uses it.

The controller draws very little power, sits behind your inverter or a small UPS, and holds the entire state of your home: every device, every rule, every event. When connectivity returns after an outage, it syncs. It never waits for permission to do its job.

Perception

Sensors, cameras, meters and device states continuously read and interpreted.

Reasoning

A local AI engine decides what a situation means and what should happen next.

Action

Lights, geysers, pumps, blinds, gates and notifications driven through one control layer.

Memory

History, routines and preferences stored privately on-site — the house learns your household.

Why local AI matters

Cloud smart-home platforms send your camera footage and household telemetry to a third party for processing. That's convenient for them and expensive for you — in privacy, in bandwidth, and in reliability the moment either end of the connection fails.

On an on-premises install, Jaxtech runs the models that matter on the box in your house. Person and vehicle recognition, presence detection and consumption analysis all execute locally — nothing about your family's movements needs to leave the property for the system to be intelligent.

Being straight about the exception: large language models are heavy. Where a project needs the reasoning of a big model, that part usually runs on hosted cloud inference — including the Tiny chat on the home page of this site. Running a big model on-premises is possible and sometimes the right call for sensitive work, but it costs more and runs slower, so it's a decision we make deliberately rather than a promise made by default.

Where cloud services genuinely add value — weather forecasts, remote access when you're away, message delivery — they're used as inputs, never as a single point of failure.

A worked example

What "one coordinated system" actually looks like on an ordinary Tuesday evening:

17:42

Grid drops. The controller detects the transfer to inverter and immediately holds the geyser and pool pump — protecting your battery reserve without you touching anything.

17:43

Fibre router loses power. Cameras keep recording and analysing locally; the outside lights still respond to presence because none of that logic lives in the cloud.

18:10

A vehicle stops at the gate. On-site recognition identifies it as unknown, so you get one clear message with a snapshot — not a generic motion alert.

21:30

Power returns. Queued notifications sync, the geyser reschedules to heat in the cheapest window before your morning shower, and irrigation checks tomorrow's forecast before committing.

06:00

Your morning briefing arrives on WhatsApp: what happened overnight, what the outage cost you, and what the house is planning today.

The engineering behind it

Jaxtech isn't a reseller putting someone else's box on your wall. The automation software, the tuning of the models that run on the edge hardware, and the integrations that make devices from a dozen incompatible brands behave like one product are all built here — by one person, which is why the scope of a project is always agreed honestly upfront.

Machine learning & edge AI

Model fine-tuning, quantisation and deployment onto local hardware — including NVIDIA edge accelerators — so inference is fast and private.

Systems & software engineering

Full-stack development, automated testing and version-controlled deployment behind every install, so updates don't break your home.

Device & protocol integration

Deep integration work across cameras, inverters, meters, gate motors, sensors and lighting — regardless of manufacturer.

Security engineering

Threat modelling, network segmentation and auditing applied to your home network, because a connected house is an attack surface.

Data & reporting

Consumption analysis and reporting that turns raw meter data into decisions you can act on.

Automation & workflow design

The discipline of turning "the geyser annoys me" into a reliable rule that works every day without supervision.

Ownership and honesty

The hardware is yours. The system runs whether or not you keep paying us. Automations are documented in plain language so you can see exactly what your house does and why, and anything consequential — gates, locks, spending — waits for a human decision by design, not as a setting you have to find.

We'd rather install a smaller system that you trust completely than a bigger one you quietly stop using.
