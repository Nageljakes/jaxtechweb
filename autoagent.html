How it works

Three parts. Nothing to rip out.

Your phone stays yours

Customer chats live on the salesperson’s companion number. The system reads along passively and drafts replies from live stock — in Afrikaans or English.

The diary works itself

Today, overdue and completed lists triaged daily. Single moves carry a note; bulk shifts preview first, verify every entry, and keep a one-command undo.

Everything gets logged twice

Each touchpoint lands in the diary and the permanent customer record. Entries move dates — they are never deleted, and booked appointments are never auto-touched.

The rails

What it never does

Never messages a customer

No auto-replies, no follow-up texts, no check-in bots. Every word a customer receives was written — or tapped send — by a human.

Never deletes a diary entry

Entries move dates with a full audit trail. The tools have no delete path at all, so lost follow-ups stop existing.

Never moves a booking

Appointments, test drives and deliveries are guarded — listed for human review, never auto-shifted by a rebalance.

Never leaks customer data

Names and numbers are masked in every report, and triage can run on local hardware so prospect data never leaves the building.

Questions a CEO asks

Straight answers.

Does it message our customers?

No — never on its own, and there is no setting that changes that. Drafts are prepared internally and a salesperson presses send from their own number. If the co-pilot is off, nothing goes anywhere: silence is the default.

Will my team actually keep the CRM updated?

That's the point. Nobody learns a new system — after a call the salesperson sends one message to the co-pilot and the logging happens: diary moved, both records written, to standard, in the moment. Data quality rises because capturing takes 30 seconds, not ten minutes at end of day. Management finally sees complete reports instead of gaps.

Does it replace our CRM?

No. It works your existing dealer CRM diary lists — today, overdue, completed — through the same action screens your staff use. No migration, no new system to learn, no rip-and-replace.

What stops it moving the wrong thing?

Three rails: bulk moves preview first and only execute on explicit approval; every move is verified entry-by-entry with a one-command undo; booked appointments, test drives and deliveries are guarded and listed for human review instead.

Who sees our customer data?

Triage and drafting can run on local models on your own hardware, so prospect data never leaves the building. Names and numbers are masked in every report. What I see during a pilot is what you show me — nothing more.

What does it cost?

Scoped fixed, quoted before any work starts — the pilot first, then per-salesperson rollout. Running costs (model usage, hosting) stay yours and are estimated honestly upfront. No hourly creep.
