Local AI fleet Models running on hardware I own

An RTX 3060 box serves the fleet through LM Studio, fronted by a role router on tiny (collective/code, collective/crm). One GPU model resident at a time, 5-minute idle eviction, smoke-tested Oct 2026. No data leaves the building unless we choose cloud for a job.

Home automation Bedroom light + face-gate cameras, live

mmWave occupancy + lux drives the bedroom light with sunrise fade and manual override. Two ONVIF cameras run Frigate with face recognition + plates; known faces stay quiet, unknown ones send one Telegram photo. 49 + 33 unit tests, systemd services, logs to prove it.

Dealership OS Prospect diaries that don’t slip

Today, overdue and completed lists triaged daily — single follow-ups moved with a verified note, overloaded days shifted or rebalanced across the week with a preview first. Every touchpoint lands in the diary and the permanent customer record. Moves only, never deletes. Plus WhatsApp drafting from live stock, in Afrikaans and English. See it work.
