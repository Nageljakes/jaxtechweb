Questions

The things everyone asks.

Does it really keep working during load-shedding?

Yes — that's the design goal. The controller and essential sensors draw very little power and sit behind your inverter or a small dedicated UPS. Because the decision-making AI runs on-site rather than in a data centre, it doesn't need your fibre to be up either. During an outage the house keeps monitoring, logging and automating; anything that needs the internet queues up and syncs the moment it's back.

Do I have to replace my existing smart devices?

Almost never. The whole point is unification — we bring your existing cameras, lights, gate motor, inverter, alarm and sensors into one system regardless of brand. We only recommend replacing something when it's genuinely closed off or unreliable, and we'll tell you why before you spend anything.

What does it cost, honestly?

It depends entirely on the size of the property and what's already installed, which is why the assessment is free and the quote is fixed. You'll get a written scope and a single number before any hardware is ordered — no hourly creep, and no "we found something" invoices afterwards.

Is there a monthly fee?

No mandatory one. The system runs on your hardware and belongs to you. Ongoing support and remote tuning are available as an optional plan, but if you cancel it the house keeps working exactly as it did.

How private is the camera AI?

Video is analysed on the controller inside your home. Footage isn't uploaded to a third-party cloud for processing, and you decide what — if anything — leaves the property. That's a deliberate architectural choice, not a setting.

How disruptive is the installation?

Most installs take a day or two and use existing wiring and DB space. We work around your routine, keep the site clean, and won't leave until you've been walked through the system yourself.

Where do you install?

Gauteng and surrounding areas as standard, with estate and business installations further afield by arrangement. Tell us where you are in the form and we'll confirm straight away.

What if I only want one part of it?

That's completely normal, and usually the smartest way to start. Plenty of homes begin with energy monitoring and geyser control alone, then add security or water later. The system is built to grow.
